# Towards a comprehensive gene regulatory network in *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*
## Ruihao Li<sup>1</sup>, William K. M. Lai<sup>1</sup>, B. Franklin Pugh<sup>1</sup>
<sup>1</sup>Department of Molecular Biology and Genetics, Cornell University, Ithaca, New York, 14853, USA
### Abstract
Gene regulatory networks (GRNs) orchestrate gene expression, enabling cellular responses critical for differentiation, development, and maintain stable transcriptional states, yet most GRN inference methods are evaluated by static overlap with binding data rather than by their ability to explain transcriptional states as emergent dynamical behaviors. Here we present IAGREE (Integrated Attractor-constrained Gene Regulatory nEtwork inferencE), a framework that infers dynamical GRNs constrained to reproduce observed transcriptional profiles as stable attractors. IAGREE identifies discrete expression states from RNA-seq data using a robust hybrid clustering strategy and infers GRNs whose dynamics recover these states.

Applying IAGREE to bulk RNA-seq data from wild-type and single transcription factor knockout  strains in *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, we show that inferred GRNs accurately reproduce all observed profiles and generalize to unseen states in cross-validation. Incorporating TF–DNA binding and protein–protein colocalization priors improves interpretability but reveals a tradeoff: overly strict constraints reduce dynamical fidelity by excluding indirect regulatory interactions. Gene-wise analyses identify loci whose multi-state behavior cannot be explained by direct TF binding alone. We further demonstrate applicability to single-cell RNA-seq data and benchmark against Inferelator 3.0. Together, IAGREE provides a systems-level framework for learning GRNs that explain transcriptional states through dynamics.
